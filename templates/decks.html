Custom Card Decks

Upload your own card decks via Discord and play with them in-game. Pro feature — each deck is private to you.

How to Upload a Deck

1

Prepare your CSV file

Create a CSV with type and text columns.

2

Upload in Discord

Use !deck upload My Deck Name and attach the CSV file to your message.

3

Play with your deck

Use !deck use My Deck Name to activate it for your next game.

CSV Format Example:

type,text
prompt,Why did _ get banned from the store?
prompt,I can't believe _ happened at the wedding.
answer,A suspiciously large bag of cheese
answer,My therapist's worst nightmare
answer,Three raccoons in a trenchcoat

type: prompt (or black/question) for prompts, answer (or white/response) for answers.
text: The card text. Use _ for blanks in prompts.

Look Up Your Decks

Enter your Discord User ID to view your uploaded decks.

To find your Discord User ID: enable Developer Mode in Discord settings, then right-click your name and select "Copy User ID".

Discord Commands (Pro only)

!deck upload <name> Upload a CSV deck (attach the file to your message)
!deck list View your uploaded card decks
!deck use <name> Use one of your decks for your next game
!deck ai Switch back to AI-generated cards
!deck info <name> See details about one of your decks
!deck delete <name> Delete one of your decks

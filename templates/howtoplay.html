How to Play

Everything you need to dominate Evil Apples 4D — from your first game to tournament glory.

What is Evil Apples 4D?

Evil Apples 4D is a Cards Against Humanity-style party game played entirely through Discord. Each round, a prompt card is revealed and players submit their funniest, most outrageous answer card. A rotating judge picks the winner, and Dr. Evil — our AI judge powered by Venice.ai — delivers savage commentary on every submission.

Play with friends in multiplayer, challenge AI opponents in solo mode, or climb the ranks in multi-round tournaments. Your wins are tracked on the live leaderboard and Hall of Fame.

Getting Started

1

Invite the Bot

Add Evil Apples 4D to your Discord server. The bot handles everything — dealing cards, managing rounds, and tracking scores.

2

Start a Game

Type !startgame to create a game, !join to hop in, then !deal to start a round. Or type !solo for solo play against AI.

3

Play & Win

Submit your funniest card, wait for the judge's pick, and earn points. Climb the leaderboard and enter the Hall of Fame!

Round-by-Round Gameplay

1

The Prompt Appears

A black prompt card is shown to all players. It contains a fill-in-the-blank statement or question generated by Venice.ai. Each prompt is unique and unpredictable.

"The secret ingredient in my grandma's soup is ___."
2

Pick Your Card

You receive a hand of 7 answer cards. Click the button matching the card you want to submit. Choose the funniest, darkest, or most absurd answer — whatever you think will win.

A suspicious amount of glitter
Existential dread
Nicolas Cage's tears
3

Exchange Cards (Optional)

Don't like your hand? Hit the Exchange button to swap up to 3 cards for new ones. You get one exchange per round — use it wisely! You cannot exchange after submitting your answer.

4

The Judge Decides

A rotating judge reviews all anonymous submissions and picks their favorite. The judge changes each round so everyone gets a turn. After the human judge picks, Dr. Evil delivers his AI commentary — roasting submissions and crowning his own favorite.

5

Score & Repeat

The winner earns a point and bragging rights. Scores carry across rounds — keep playing to climb the live leaderboard! Type !deal again to start the next round.

Game Modes

👪

FRIEND Mode

The classic experience. Invite your friends, play at your own pace with no timer pressure. First to 7 points wins!

👥 2-20 players

🃏 7 cards per hand

🏆 First to 7 points wins

⏰ No timer — unlimited time per round

!startgame or !startgame friend
🔄

SWAP Mode

Cards are shuffled between players each round! You never know whose cards you'll get. Pure chaos and unpredictable fun.

👥 5+ players required

🃏 7 cards per hand (shuffled between players!)

🏆 First to 7 points wins

🎲 Cards rotate every round

!startgame swap
✍️

WILDCARD Mode

No pre-made cards — you write your own answers! Type whatever you want in response to each prompt. Unleash your inner comedian.

👥 5+ players required

✍️ 0 cards — write your own!

🏆 First to 7 points wins

💡 Type custom answers via a popup

!startgame wildcard
🤖

Solo Mode

Play against AI opponents when your friends are offline. Dr. Evil generates AI player submissions and judges the round. Your wins still count for the leaderboard!

!solo — Start a solo game (default 3 AI opponents)
!solo deal — Deal a solo round
!solo score — Check your solo stats
!solo end — End solo game
🏆

Tournaments

Multi-round competitions with a set number of rounds. The player with the most wins at the end takes the tournament crown. Results are saved to the Hall of Fame.

!tournament create <name> [rounds] [$fee] — Create a tournament
!tournament join — Join a tournament
!tournament start — Begin the tournament
!tournament status — View standings

Paid Tournaments

💰

Host tournaments with real entry fees. Players pay to enter, and money flows instantly to the host via Stripe Connect.

How the Money Flows

1. Player pays entry fee (e.g. $10)
2. Stripe automatically splits: $9 to host, $1 platform fee (10%)
3. Host receives funds directly in their connected Stripe account
4. Winners with connected accounts can receive prize transfers

For Tournament Hosts

  1. Connect your Stripe account (button below)
  2. Create a tournament with a fee: !tournament create MyTourney 5 $10
  3. Players join and pay on the website
  4. Run the tournament — payout happens automatically when it ends

For Players

  1. Type !tournament join in Discord
  2. The bot gives you a payment link
  3. Pay the entry fee via Stripe
  4. You're automatically registered once payment confirms

Connect Your Stripe Account

To host paid tournaments, you need to connect a Stripe account so we can send you your share of the prize pool.

Pay Tournament Entry Fee

Enter the Tournament ID and your Discord User ID to pay an entry fee.

Card Decks & Customization

🍴

Default Deck

Every game comes with 124+ built-in answer cards for free. No setup needed — just start playing.

🛒

Deck Store

Browse and purchase themed expansion decks from the Deck Store. Each deck adds unique cards to your games — from Surreal to Pop Culture to Tech Bro themes.

📄

Custom CSV Uploads

Create your own card deck by uploading a CSV file. Each line becomes an answer card. Manage your custom decks on the Decks page.

💡

Selecting Decks

Each player picks their own decks! When you start or join a game, you'll be prompted to select up to 3 of your decks. Cards from your decks are dealt only to you — other players get their own deck cards or default cards. Use !decks to view or change your selection, or !deck use <name> to quick-select a deck.

Dr. Evil — Your AI Judge

😈

Every round, Dr. Evil (powered by Cyberdyne Systems Skynet technology) reviews all submissions and delivers unfiltered commentary. He'll roast bad answers, praise creative ones, and pick his own favorite — separate from the human judge's choice.

Dr. Evil's style is sharp, witty, and unapologetically savage. Don't take it personally... unless your card really was that bad.

Tips & Strategy

🎯 Know Your Judge

Each judge has different taste. Pay attention to what wins each round and tailor your picks to whoever's judging.

💫 Save Your Best Cards

Don't blow your strongest card on a weak prompt. Wait for the perfect combo and play it when it counts.

♻️ Use Your Exchange

If your hand is garbage, swap up to 3 cards before submitting. One exchange per round — make it count.

🏆 Play Tournaments

Tournaments reward consistency over multiple rounds. Pace yourself and stay focused across all rounds to claim the crown.

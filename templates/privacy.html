Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Evil Apples 4D ("we", "us", "our") operates the website evilapples4d.com and the Evil Apples 4D Discord bot. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

2. Information We Collect

Discord Account Data

When you interact with our Discord bot or log in via Discord OAuth, we receive and store your Discord user ID, username, and avatar URL. We do not access your email address, friend list, or private messages.

Game Data

We store gameplay statistics including rounds played, rounds won, cards submitted, tournament results, and leaderboard rankings. This data is linked to your Discord user ID.

Payment Information

If you purchase premium features, deck packs, or enter paid tournaments, payment processing is handled entirely by Stripe. We do not store your credit card number, bank account details, or other financial credentials. We store only the Stripe customer ID and transaction metadata (amounts, timestamps, and status) for record-keeping.

Server and Guild Data

We store Discord server (guild) IDs and channel IDs where the bot is active. This is used to manage game sessions, premium status, and tournament associations.

Automatically Collected Data

When you visit our website, our server logs may record your IP address, browser type, and pages visited. This data is used for security, debugging, and aggregate analytics only.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and improve the Evil Apples 4D game experience
  • To track scores, leaderboards, and tournament standings
  • To process payments and manage premium subscriptions
  • To manage custom card decks and store purchases
  • To display ads to non-premium users
  • To detect abuse, cheating, and unauthorized use
  • To communicate important service updates

4. Third-Party Services

Discord

We use Discord's API and OAuth2 for authentication and bot functionality. Your use of Discord is governed by Discord's Privacy Policy.

Stripe

Payment processing is handled by Stripe. When you make a payment, your financial information is transmitted directly to Stripe and is subject to Stripe's Privacy Policy.

Venice AI

We use Venice.ai's API to generate card content, prompts, and AI judge commentary. Game prompts and player-submitted text may be sent to Venice.ai for processing. Venice.ai does not receive your Discord identity or personal information.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Replit's infrastructure. We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), secure session management, and access controls. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your game data and account information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. If you wish to have your data deleted, please contact us (see below). Payment records may be retained as required by law or for legitimate business purposes (e.g., tax records, dispute resolution).

7. Your Rights

  • Access: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: You can request that we correct inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: You can request that we delete your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Opt-out: You can stop using the bot at any time by removing it from your server or ceasing interaction with it.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

8. Children's Privacy

Evil Apples 4D is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Your continued use of Evil Apples 4D after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
